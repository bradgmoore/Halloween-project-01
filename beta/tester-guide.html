Your test session

  1. Open the beta app in Safari or Chrome. Use the beta link for all testing.
  2. Open Record & assign, enter your first name or nickname, and give your clip a useful name.
  3. Use Choose / check microphone to identify your phone/tablet mic. Record a short original phrase, cackle, whisper or spooky voice. Try Natural, Witch, Zombie, Ghoul, Demon or Ghost. Speak only with permission from anyone whose voice you record.
  4. Finish and preview your take. It is automatically saved in Recording inbox on this browser. You can also assign it to a button.
  5. In Recording inbox, mark your best takes Keep. Use Export keepers or Share recordings with organiser, then send the file to the person who gave you this link. Ask them to confirm receipt.
  6. Try the Sound effects and Music pages, add a downloaded audio file, and test hold-to-talk. On release, the microphone should close. Use your own headphones/speaker for testing; the event setup uses an iPhone paired directly to the Bluetooth speaker.
  7. Send a short report with your device, browser, what worked, what failed, and any delay or unexpected mic/speaker routing.

Save before leaving: recordings are local to this browser. The organiser cannot see them until you send an export. Do not clear browser data before your files are received. Beta reset buttons preserve the recording inbox, but clearing site data does not.

What happens to good recordings?

The organiser imports tester files into their beta inbox, reviews and marks keepers, and assigns approved clips to the final twelve buttons. A final setup file is then transferred to the Lenovo. Sound effects and Music remain separate from the custom buttons.

This beta currently uses file sharing rather than automatic cloud upload. A secure admin login and central inbox are planned as a separate backend-connected stage.
