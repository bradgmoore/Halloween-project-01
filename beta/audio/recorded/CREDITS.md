# Recorded scare phrases

Voice pack by Kenney. Source repository supplies the audio under CC0 1.0 Universal; legal text included in LICENSE-CC0.txt.

Reviewed source: https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-voiceover-fighter-for-godot/tree/08f93f0697459505e83f351f0e532115ca0550da

Sixteen source phrases across two voices are included, each in original and pitch/echo-processed Demon form (32 clips). These are spoken phrases, not scream/laughter recordings. No third-party files with unverifiable redistribution rights were included. Source filenames and SHA-256 checksums are in sources.json.

Conversion and processing are reproducible with tools/import_recorded_voices.py.
