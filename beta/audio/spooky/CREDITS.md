# Ring for Candy Halloween library

64 original procedural audio compositions generated with tools/generate_library.py. No third-party recordings, film samples, or human voice recordings are used. Ghost/creature/voice descriptions refer to synthesized tones and textures. This is not a third-party hosted sound archive.
